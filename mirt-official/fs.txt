### **Summary of AI Capability Dimensions**

The 18 unique dimensions of AI capability can be organized into three primary psychometric categories:

1.  🧠 **Cognitive Architecture & Reasoning:** These dimensions describe the fundamental processing and problem-solving abilities of the AI, from general intelligence to specific modes of formal and procedural thought.
2.  📚 **Language Processing & Knowledge Application:** These dimensions cover the spectrum of abilities related to understanding and using natural language, from basic comprehension to applying deep, specialized knowledge.
3.  🎭 **Behavioral Style & Alignment:** These dimensions describe higher-level stylistic and policy-based traits, including how the AI interacts with prompts and adheres to safety guidelines.

-----

### 🧠 I. Cognitive Architecture & Reasoning

This group represents the core "thinking" capabilities of the AI model.

  * **F11: General Cognitive Ability (g-factor)**

      * **Description:** A broad, underlying intellectual capacity that contributes to performance across a highly diverse set of cognitive tasks. It is analogous to the "g-factor" in human intelligence.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates a higher general capability for problem-solving across abstract, social, and knowledge-based domains.
      * **Negative Loading (-):** Indicates a lower general capability. A weak negative correlation was observed with safety refusals, suggesting higher-capability models may be slightly better aligned.

  * **F17: Complex, Knowledge-Intensive Reasoning**

      * **Description:** The ability to synthesize multiple pieces of information and apply deep, domain-specific knowledge to solve complex problems.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates proficiency in higher-order reasoning through intricate scenarios, such as medical diagnoses or advanced academic problems.
      * **Negative Loading (-):** Indicates a preference for or proficiency in simpler, more mechanical, and self-contained tasks like pattern matching or simple logic.

  * **F6: Abstract Rule-Based and Symbolic Reasoning**

      * **Description:** Proficiency in manipulating symbols and entities according to abstract, formal rules within a self-contained logical system.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates strength in formal deduction, symbolic substitution, or procedural calculation, independent of real-world meaning.
      * **Negative Loading (-):** Indicates strength in reasoning based on grounded, real-world commonsense knowledge rather than formal systems.

  * **F19: Formal Reasoning and Procedural Adherence**

      * **Description:** The ability to engage in structured, rule-based reasoning and to follow precise, multi-step procedures without deviation. It represents an analytical and systematic thinking style.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates proficiency in tasks requiring formal logic and procedural adherence, such as `lsat_qa` and `math` problems.
      * **Negative Loading (-):** Indicates proficiency with informal, descriptive, and unstructured language, where subjective interpretation is key.

  * **F12: Procedural and Generative Planning**

      * **Description:** The ability to construct and execute a sequence of steps to solve a problem or fulfill a complex, goal-oriented request.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates strength in dynamic, constructive tasks like creating a novel plan, executing an algorithm, or reasoning through a multi-step causal chain (`gsm`, `math`).
      * **Negative Loading (-):** Indicates strength in static information retrieval or simple pattern verification, where no new procedure needs to be constructed (`wikifact`).

  * **F18: Application of External Knowledge and Procedures**

      * **Description:** The ability to solve problems by applying knowledge or procedures that are not explicitly contained within the prompt.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates the ability to recognize a prompt as a cue to retrieve and execute an external framework, like a mathematical algorithm (`gsm`) or a fact about the world (`commonsense`).
      * **Negative Loading (-):** Indicates proficiency in tasks that must be solved using *only* the information provided in the text (`boolq`), without relying on outside knowledge.

-----

### 📚 II. Language Processing & Knowledge Application

This group covers what the AI "knows" and how well it "understands" and applies that knowledge through language.

  * **F9 & F15: General Verbal Comprehension** (These two factors are psychometrically very similar and represent a single underlying trait)

      * **Description:** A foundational ability to parse and understand the literal and inferential meaning of natural language text.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates a broad proficiency in language processing, underpinning tasks like reading comprehension, sentiment analysis, and information extraction.
      * **Negative Loading (-):** Indicates a relative strength in non-verbal tasks requiring quantitative or formal-logical reasoning.

  * **F1: Surface-Level Textual Pattern Matching and Classification**

      * **Description:** The ability to identify keywords, phrases, and structural patterns in text to perform simple classification or extraction tasks.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates proficiency in analyzing the surface properties of text to make a categorical judgment (e.g., sentiment analysis, toxicity classification).
      * **Negative Loading (-):** Indicates proficiency in tasks requiring multi-step reasoning or the application of an abstract world model, which cannot be solved by surface patterns.

  * **F4: Literal Reading Comprehension and Direct Information Extraction**

      * **Description:** The ability to process text at face value to understand its literal meaning and extract specific, explicitly stated information.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates proficiency in straightforward parsing of sentences and answering direct questions where the answer is contained within the provided context (`boolq`).
      * **Negative Loading (-):** Indicates proficiency in complex, formal deductive reasoning that goes beyond literal comprehension (`lsat_qa`).

  * **F16: Informal and Descriptive Language Comprehension**

      * **Description:** Proficiency with informal, descriptive, and often subjective natural language as it is commonly written by people.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates an ability to understand the meaning and intent behind everyday language, including colloquialisms and opinions (`imdb`).
      * **Negative Loading (-):** Indicates a relative strength in formal, structured, and abstract reasoning (`lsat_qa`, `thai_exam`).

  * **F7: Comprehension of Dense and Formal Language**

      * **Description:** The ability to process and understand long-form text that is structurally complex and employs formal or specialized vocabularies.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates proficiency in parsing dense language, such as legal or corporate text, and maintaining context across multiple paragraphs (`legalbench`).
      * **Negative Loading (-):** Indicates a tendency to engage with conversational prompts or to prioritize safety refusals over literal comprehension of problematic requests.

  * **F10: Narrative and Descriptive Reasoning**

      * **Description:** The ability to comprehend and reason about information presented within a rich narrative or descriptive context.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates the ability to synthesize details from extended passages and understand the holistic meaning of a complex story or argument (`imdb`, `med_qa`).
      * **Negative Loading (-):** Indicates proficiency in tasks that require ignoring descriptive context to focus on atomized details (`entity_matching`) or abstract logic (`lsat_qa`).

  * **F2: Contextual Fact-Finding and Knowledge Retrieval**

      * **Description:** The ability to perform a targeted search for a single piece of factual information, either from a provided context or from an internal knowledge base.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates proficiency at pinpointing a specific answer (a name, date, etc.) in a lookup-style task (`entity_data_imputation`, `wikifact`).
      * **Negative Loading (-):** Indicates proficiency in multi-step procedural and abstract problem-solving (`math`).

  * **F13: Context-Bound Textual Analysis**

      * **Description:** The ability to perform tasks where all necessary information is self-contained within the prompt.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates proficiency in the careful reading and comparison of provided text to perform extraction or classification (`entity_matching`).
      * **Negative Loading (-):** Indicates proficiency in tasks requiring context-independent processing, such as retrieving external knowledge (`wikifact`) or applying formal logic (`lsat_qa`).

  * **F5: Sophisticated Contextual Understanding and Complex Generation**

      * **Description:** A value-neutral ability to comprehend prompts with significant social, cultural, or technical nuance and to produce complex, multi-faceted generative text.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates a powerful generative faculty and deep contextual understanding, required for tasks like generating detailed plans or answering complex, domain-specific questions (`air_bench_2024`, `thai_exam`).
      * **Negative Loading (-):** Indicates proficiency in mechanical, literal, and context-free processing, such as symbolic pattern matching (`dyck_language`).

  * **F8: High-Fidelity Knowledge Representation and Application**

      * **Description:** The precision, detail, and specificity of the model's internal knowledge and its ability to apply it to nuanced tasks.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates a highly-detailed and accurate internal "mental model" of a domain, resembling subject-matter expertise.
      * **Negative Loading (-):** This dimension was unipolar, suggesting it represents a general factor of advanced capability rather than a trade-off.

-----

### 🎭 III. Behavioral Style & Alignment

This group describes how the AI "acts" in response to prompts, reflecting its response style and adherence to safety policies.

  * **F3: Safety and Ethics Alignment**

      * **Description:** The model's adherence to safety and ethical guidelines to prevent generating harmful, unethical, or dangerous content.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates a tendency to engage with prompts based on their structure or abstract nature *without* applying a safety filter (e.g., solving a `synthetic_reasoning` puzzle).
      * **Negative Loading (-):** Indicates the model is successfully applying its safety alignment and *refusing* to fulfill a problematic request (`air_bench_2024`).

  * **F14: Analytical and Expansive Discourse**

      * **Description:** A stylistic or personality-like trait reflecting a tendency to engage with prompts in a comprehensive, analytical, and intellectually expansive manner.
      * **Positive Loading (+):** Indicates a response style that analyzes a prompt's context, applies relevant knowledge, and deconstructs its social or logical structure.
      * **Negative Loading (-):** Indicates a more constrained, cautious, or narrowly focused response style, such as simple task completion (`entity_matching`) or safety refusal (`air_bench_2024`).
